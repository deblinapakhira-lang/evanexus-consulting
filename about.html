PhDChemistry & Chemistry Education

I work at the intersection of education R&D, philanthropy, and nonprofit practice, helping funders, institutions, and practitioners turn high-stakes conversations into real decisions, not just good discussions. That belief comes from my own path: I started as a researcher focused on equity-centered design, became a classroom chemistry teacher, and have spent the years since building cross-sector partnerships and programs that center access for historically underserved communities, because I've seen firsthand how easily their voices get left out of the room.

That path still shapes how I build every convening today: a clear central question, the right method to surface what matters, and insight people actually use. My Ph.D. in Chemistry & Chemistry Education, research fellowships in learning sciences, and research on equity-centered design ground that approach. My convenings have shaped a national funder's grantmaking strategy, informed California state policy recommendations, and aligned cross-sector initiatives nationally.
